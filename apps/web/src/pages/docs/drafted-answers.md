> Drafted answers are part of **Pro**. See [plans and billing](/docs/plans).

## Where it works

On the same forms as [autofill](/docs/autofill): Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, including forms embedded in a company's careers page. Questions like "Why do you want to work here?" are open questions, which autofill leaves alone.

## Using it

1. Click into an open question on the form. A small **jlog · Draft** button appears in its corner.
2. Press it. jlog drafts an answer and writes it into the field.
3. Underneath, it lists what the draft is based on: each fact from your CV with the role it came from, and any line of the posting it answers.
4. Edit the draft as you like, and submit the form yourself.

## What it uses

- **Your facts**, from your [imported CV](/docs/cv).
- **The posting.** When the application is already in jlog, its saved job description; otherwise the text of the page.
- **Your AI provider** from Settings.

## When there is not enough to go on

If your facts do not support an answer to the question, jlog says what is missing instead of writing something generic or invented.
