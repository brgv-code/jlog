question: How do jlog, Huntr and Simplify compare for tracking job applications?
answer: All three track applications and fill forms from a Chrome extension. Simplify fills forms on far more sites and can submit for you with its paid Autopilot; Huntr adds a resume builder and AI credits in a subscription; jlog is free and open source, runs its AI on your own key, and never submits.
updated: 2026-10-30
faqs:
  - q: Which is cheapest?
    a: All three have a free tier. On 30 October 2026 Huntr Pro was $40 a month and Simplify+ was $39.99 a month, each with cheaper weekly or multi-month options. jlog has no paid plan today; its AI features run on your own provider key, which usually costs under €2 for a whole job search.
  - q: Which one fills the most application forms?
    a: Simplify. Its Copilot says it autofills applications on 100,000+ company career sites. jlog fills Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby forms, including ones embedded in a company's careers page.
  - q: Do any of them submit applications for me?
    a: Simplify's paid Autopilot fills and submits without a review step. Its free Copilot leaves the submit to you. jlog never submits. Huntr's pricing page does not say either way.
  - q: Which is open source or self-hostable?
    a: jlog is MIT licensed and can run on your own Cloudflare account. Neither Huntr's nor Simplify's pages say they are open source or self-hostable.

## Side by side

Facts about Huntr and Simplify are from their own pages, read on 30 October 2026. Where a page did not say, the table says so rather than guessing. Prices change; check the source before deciding.

| | jlog | Huntr | Simplify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free tier | Every feature | Tracker up to 100 jobs, unlimited autofills, 2 tailored resumes, a small number of AI credits | Copilot autofill, job tracker, resume builder, Gmail integration |
| Paid plan | None today | Pro: $40/month, or $90 per 3 months, or $160 per 6 months | Simplify+: $19.99/week, $39.99/month, or $89.99 per 3 months |
| Chrome extension | Records applications on 6 boards, fills forms | Job clipper, fills forms | Copilot: fills forms, saves to the tracker |
| Where it fills forms | Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby (also embedded) | Not stated | 100,000+ company career sites |
| Submits for you | Never | Not stated | Only with Autopilot (Simplify+), no review step |
| AI tailoring and cover letters | Free, on your own AI key | 2 tailored resumes free; unlimited AI credits on Pro | Simplify+ |
| Use your own AI key or Ollama | Yes | Not stated | Not stated |
| Open source, self-hostable | Yes, MIT | Not stated | Not stated |

Sources: [Huntr pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing), [Simplify Copilot](https://simplify.jobs/copilot), [What's included in Simplify+](https://help.simplify.jobs/articles/5623502-whats-included-in-simplify). For jlog: [plans](/docs/plans), [autofill](/docs/autofill), [capture](/docs/capture).

## Choose Simplify if

- you want autofill on as many sites as possible: its 100,000+ career sites is far wider than jlog's three form providers;
- you want applications submitted for you, and are happy to pay for Autopilot and skip the review step.

## Choose Huntr if

- you want a resume builder with templates and AI credits in one subscription, without setting up an AI provider key;
- a tracker capped at 100 jobs on the free tier is enough, or you plan to pay for Pro.

## Choose jlog if

- you want to read every form before it goes, and a tool that never presses submit;
- you would rather pay an AI provider at cost than a subscription, or run a model yourself with Ollama;
- you want the code, MIT licensed, and the option to [run it on your own Cloudflare account](/open-source-job-tracker);
- you want each tailored CV line to be a sentence you already wrote, with its source shown.

## What jlog does not do yet

Autofill covers Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, not every careers site. There is no auto-apply, by design. The paid cloud plan, with the AI included so you need no key, is not out yet.
